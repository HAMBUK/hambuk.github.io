The core idea

Let the signal decide
when to compute.

SpikeMamba maps selective state space dynamics onto spiking neurons. Sparse binary events carry information, while a state-dependent gate suppresses updates during inactive segments.

01 / Method

A state space model.
A spiking core.

Continuous inputs. Binary events.
Selective recurrent updates.

01

Encode the signal

A linear projection, batch normalization, and SiLU prepare continuous biomedical signals. Depthwise convolution mixes local temporal information.

02

Update through spikes

Leaky Integrate-and-Fire dynamics maintain a membrane state and emit binary spikes. Downstream spike-weight products become conditional accumulations.

03

Gate inactive steps

A state-dependent gate uses firing activity and membrane-potential variance to determine whether to update or preserve the state.

Energy accounting

E = EMAC · FLInput+MLP + EAC · Reff · FLSpike

Reff = firing rate × gate activation ratio
